# Privacy Policy for SnapQuery

## Data Collection and Usage

SnapQuery is designed with privacy in mind. Here's how we handle your data:

### What We Collect
- **Content Analysis**: The extension analyzes webpage content, images, and PDFs to provide AI responses
- **User Questions**: Your questions and interactions with the AI assistant
- **Technical Data**: Basic usage statistics and error logs

### What We DON'T Collect
- Personal identification information
- Browsing history outside of current page
- Login credentials or passwords
- Personal files or documents

### How We Use Your Data
- Process your questions and provide AI-powered responses
- Improve extension functionality and performance
- Debug technical issues

### Data Storage
- **Local Processing**: Content extraction happens in your browser
- **Temporary Processing**: Data is sent to AI services for analysis and immediately discarded
- **No Permanent Storage**: We do not store your content or conversations

### Third-Party Services
- **OpenRouter API**: Used for AI responses (see their privacy policy)
- **OCR Services**: Used for image text extraction
- **Backend Services**: Temporary processing only

### Your Rights
- You can uninstall the extension at any time
- No account required - completely anonymous usage
- All data processing is temporary and non-persistent
